The things people ask before they make their first clip.

Is Klipu free?

Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on how many videos you clip.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs inside your browser, on your own device. Your video never leaves it.

Which videos work best?

Anything where people talk: interviews, podcasts, talks, lessons and vlogs. English, Hindi and Bengali speech can be written as captions.

How long are the clips, and what shape?

Clips run 30 or 60 seconds. Choose vertical 9:16 for Shorts, Reels and TikTok, or square, portrait, wide or the original shape.

Can I paste a YouTube link?

Not yet. A browser cannot fetch YouTube videos on its own, so save the video to your phone first and add the file here.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, it is made for phones. Keep the screen on while Klipu works, and you can add it to your home screen as an app.
